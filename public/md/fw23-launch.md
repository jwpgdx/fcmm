The MUGLER x GENTLE MONSTER collaboration integrates MUGLER’s unique heritage and contemporary signature spiral details with GENTLE MONSTER’s aesthetics, resulting in two unique silhouettes.

<br><br><br><br>

## View the Collection

The collection consists of Spiral 01, which conveys an intense energy from its geometric design inspired by a modern exploration of MUGLER’s ‘FOURMIS’ eyewear first introduced as part of the House’s 1997 ‘LES INSECTES’ haute couture collection; it is launched alongside Spiral 02, which stands out with a contemporary edge, featuring embossed line details and a chrome logo on the temples.
