# Privacy Notice

**Last Updated: October 5, 2026**

This page describes the privacy-related behavior of this FCMM portfolio project. It is a non-commercial portfolio presentation and is not the current official FCMM commerce service.

## What this portfolio stores

This project does not provide an application account, authentication, payment, order, inventory, delivery, or customer database backend.

The interactive wishlist and cart demonstrations may store the following values in your browser using `localStorage`:

- `fcmm-wishes` — product identifiers saved to the wishlist demo
- `fcmm-cart` — cart demo items and their selected interface options

These values are used only to preserve the demo state in the same browser. The application does not intentionally submit them to an FCMM application backend.

You can remove this local demo state by clearing site data in your browser.

## Information you enter

The public portfolio build does not provide a working member registration or login service. Login-related screens are demonstrations only.

Do not enter real passwords, payment information, or other sensitive personal information into this portfolio.

## Hosting and third-party content

The site is delivered through web-hosting infrastructure, so ordinary network requests required to load the site may be handled by the hosting provider.

Some pages may also load third-party embedded content, such as a map. When third-party content is requested, the third-party provider may receive ordinary request information under its own terms and privacy practices. This portfolio does not use those requests to create an FCMM customer account or order record.

## Cookies and advertising

This portfolio does not intentionally implement an FCMM advertising, marketing-profile, payment, or member-tracking system.

This notice does not make claims about cookies or request logs that may be created independently by browser, hosting, or third-party infrastructure.

Brand, product, and campaign assets remain the property of their respective owners and are presented to demonstrate design and frontend implementation work.

For the project boundary and credits, see **Project Info** in the site footer.
