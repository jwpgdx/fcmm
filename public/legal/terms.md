## Demonstration only

The public portfolio does not provide live FCMM commerce operations.

In particular, it does not provide:

- member registration or authentication
- real ordering or checkout
- payment processing
- live inventory or stock management
- shipping or delivery operations
- commercial returns or refunds

Wishlist, cart, login, product-option, and related interactions are interface demonstrations. Adding an item to the bag does not create an order or purchase contract.

Prices, colours, sizes, availability, campaign dates, and other catalogue information shown in the portfolio may be historical, illustrative, or presented for interface demonstration unless explicitly verified otherwise.
